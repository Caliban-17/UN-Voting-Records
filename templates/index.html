07 · The emergency sessions

When the Security Council is blocked, the Assembly votes

Under the 1950 Uniting for Peace resolution, an emergency special session lets the Assembly act when a veto deadlocks the Council. Two are still open: the tenth, on the occupied Palestinian territory, since 1997; the eleventh, on Ukraine, since 2022. Every recorded vote of the chosen session, in order.

Tallies are the official counts recorded with each resolution. Earlier sessions appear only where the record carries their symbols.

How to read these numbers. Only roll-call votes are recorded; resolutions adopted by consensus never appear, so every chart describes the contested end of the Assembly's work. "Taking a side" means voting Yes or No, as it does when the Assembly counts a majority. Themes are assigned from each resolution's title and UN subject headings; regional groups are the United Nations' own.
